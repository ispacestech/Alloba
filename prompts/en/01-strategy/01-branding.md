# Branding prompt

Strong brand identity is crucial for the business: the visual elements, tone and
overall personality that resonate with the target audience.

## Visual identity

- **Logo design**: create a memorable logo that reflects the brand's essence.
- **Color palette**: choose colors that evoke the desired emotions and align
  with the brand's mission.
- **Typography**: select fonts that enhance readability while embodying the
  brand's personality.

## Sustainable branding

With growing awareness of environmental issues, sustainable branding is more
crucial than ever:

- **Material selection**: use eco-friendly materials for packaging and
  promotional materials.
- **Production processes**: consider methods that minimize waste and energy
  consumption.
- **Brand messaging**: communicate the brand's commitment to sustainability
  through transparent messaging.
