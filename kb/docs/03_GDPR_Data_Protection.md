# GDPR — Data Protection Operating Notes

Practical operating notes for the platform's data protection obligations,
complementing the platform compliance document.

## Lawful bases in practice

| Processing | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- |
| Account creation and order fulfilment | Performance of a contract |
| Supplier verification (KYC) | Legal obligation / legitimate interest |
| Marketing emails | Consent (opt-in) |
| Fraud prevention | Legitimate interest (balanced) |
| AI compliance assistance | Legitimate interest / explicit consent for profiling |

## Data subject rights workflow

1. Receive the request through a single channel and verify identity.
2. Confirm receipt within 48 hours.
3. Fulfil or refuse within one month (extendable by two months with notice).
4. Log every step in the audit trail.

## Breach notification

- Notify the supervisory authority **within 72 hours** of becoming aware.
- Notify affected individuals when the risk to rights and freedoms is high.
- Keep an internal register of every breach, including near-misses.

## Processor agreements

Every processor must commit to:

- processing only on documented instructions;
- confidentiality obligations on personnel;
- appropriate technical and organisational measures;
- assisting the controller with data subject rights;
- deletion or return of data at the end of the contract;
- audit rights for the controller.

## Localisation

Where the platform operates in Africa, processing must consider local data
protection laws (e.g. the African Union Malabo Convention on Cyber Security and
Personal Data Protection) in addition to the GDPR when EU residents are
concerned.
