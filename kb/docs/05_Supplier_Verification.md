# Supplier Verification

Process notes for verifying suppliers listed in the catalog and for the
compliance checks the sourcing agent performs.

## Verification levels

| Level | Evidence | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 — Identity | Business registration, VAT/TAX id, bank account in business name | Catalog listing |
| L2 — Capacity | Product certificates, factory audit, capacity statement | Shortlisting |
| L3 — Compliance | ISO/QA certificates, export permits, sanctions screening | Cross-border deals |

## Minimum checks before listing

1. Business registration is valid and current.
2. A contactable representative is on record.
3. The supplier confirms the product lines and MOQ they advertise.
4. The supplier passes a sanctions/denied-parties screening.

## Sanctions and export controls

- Screening must cover UN, EU, US (OFAC) and relevant African frameworks.
- Records of screening are kept for at least the duration of the contract plus
  applicable retention periods.
- Automated screening results are always reviewed by a human before action.

## Ongoing monitoring

- Re-verify registered suppliers every 12 months.
- Re-run sanctions screening at each major transaction.
- Record complaints, delivery failures and quality disputes in the supplier
  profile and feed them back into the catalog ratings.
